About

Hi, I'm Connor

I'm a senior in Game Design & Interactive Media at West Virginia University and Lead Programmer on Derby at the nonprofit studio MonRiverGames.

I like systems that are small, decoupled, and composable: an NPC built from stacked components, or a world clock that broadcasts events other systems listen to instead of polling. I prototype fast to find out what's fun, then refactor the prototype into something stable that the rest of the team can build on.

My psychology background shapes how I tune those systems: I care about how they feel to the player, not only whether they work.
